Privacy Policy

Your screenshots and edits stay on your device.

Atheon Snap helps you screenshot and edit anything in your browser — in style — across X (Twitter), Threads, and the Web. The extension processes captures locally and does not upload them to Atheon.

Effective and last updated: November 1, 2026

Plain-language summary

We do not sell personal information, run behavioral advertising, or use browsing activity to determine creditworthiness. Payment is handled on Dodo Payments’ hosted checkout. Pro license validation sends only the information needed to confirm and manage a license.

1. What Atheon Snap processes

When you choose a capture or editing feature, Atheon Snap processes the visible page content and the content you select, including text, images, layout information, and screenshots. This enables page, scrolling, full-page, X (Twitter), Threads, and general Web capture and editing features.

Processing happens inside your browser. Atheon does not receive or store the content of your screenshots, edited pages, or drafts.

2. Source media and websites

Images, videos, fonts, and other media shown in a capture may be loaded directly from the website you are viewing or from that website’s content-delivery network. Those requests are governed by the privacy practices of the original website or service.

3. Information stored locally

Atheon Snap may store settings, feature preferences, drafts, rating-prompt state, and Pro license or activation state in Chrome’s local extension storage. This information stays in the Chrome profile unless a feature explicitly needs to contact the license service.

You can remove local extension data by clearing the extension’s data or uninstalling Atheon Snap.

4. Pro purchase and license validation

Pro purchases are completed on Dodo Payments’ hosted checkout. Dodo Payments acts as the Merchant of Record and handles payment, tax, receipt, refund, and related transaction information under its own privacy policy. Atheon Snap never receives your full card or bank details.

To activate, validate, or deactivate Pro, the extension sends the license key, a generated instance identifier, and a descriptive instance name to Dodo Payments’ license service. Dodo may also process associated account, purchase, device, network, and security information as described in its own policies.

5. Permissions

Atheon Snap requests only the Chrome permissions needed for its features. These may include access to the active tab, scripting, local storage, side panel, downloads, and limited access to supported sites. Optional host access for Threads is requested only when you choose to use Threads-specific features.

Permissions are used to capture and edit the page you choose, save your settings, export your work, open the side panel, and provide supported-site features. They are not used to monitor unrelated browsing.

6. Sharing, selling, and advertising

Atheon does not sell or rent personal information. We do not use extension data for personalized advertising, and we do not transfer browsing activity to data brokers. Information is shared only with service providers where necessary to deliver a feature, process a purchase, validate a license, prevent abuse, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

7. Retention and deletion

Local extension data remains in your Chrome profile until you clear it or uninstall the extension. Dodo Payments retains transaction and license records according to its legal obligations and retention policies. If you deactivate an instance, the extension requests removal of that activation from the license service.

8. Security

Network requests used for hosted checkout and license operations are sent over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely risk-free, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

Atheon Snap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the extension.

10. Chrome Web Store Limited Use

Atheon Snap’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when an update is published.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the Support section on Atheon Snap’s Chrome Web Store listing. This avoids publishing a separate personal email address.
